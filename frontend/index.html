About Our Platform

Medisync is a state-of-the-art health platform designed to connect patients directly with their local pharmacists. We eliminate the friction of lost prescriptions, missed dosages, and out-of-stock medications by bringing the entire pharmacy experience into a single, synchronized digital ecosystem.

Key Features

⏰

Smart Reminders

Never miss a dose. Patients receive daily schedules and can instantly log whether a medication was taken or skipped.

📄

Secure Prescriptions

Upload your paper prescriptions securely. Pharmacists review, verify, and automatically link them to your profile.

💳

Integrated Pharmacy POS

Pharmacists can manage live inventory, process point-of-sale transactions, and monitor real-time revenue analytics.
